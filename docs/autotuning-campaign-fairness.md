# Autotuning campaign fairness

Campaign search sees only the existing Stage 6B/6C visible frame and production outcomes. Terminal materialization is artifact-hash ordered and timing-blind. Acquisition uses the frozen Stage 7C round-robin order. Recovery decisions are server-owned observations under Stage 7D. Recommendation uses only a complete verified cohort and the frozen Stage 7B objective.

Synthetic studies are labelled `synthetic_test_data_not_performance_evidence`. They demonstrate determinism and lifecycle behavior, not hardware performance. Wall-clock observations are excluded from semantic comparisons. No measurement can advance a proof frontier or select/publish a live artifact.
