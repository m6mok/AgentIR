# Measurement acquisition fairness

One plan fixes artifact set, round-robin order, record count, target, compiler build, runtime, device fingerprint and benchmark configuration. This prevents request-order priority and cross-device/build/config pooling. Failures and unavailable devices remain visible typed outcomes rather than censored timing values.

Synthetic study records are labelled `synthetic_test_data_not_performance_evidence`; they demonstrate orchestration reproducibility only. A complete acquisition result is not a cohort, recommendation, performance proof, statistical-significance claim, portability claim, correctness evidence or globally optimal result.
